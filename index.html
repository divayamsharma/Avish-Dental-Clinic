Free Dental Wisdom

Tips From Dr. Disha's Desk

Knowledge that keeps your smile healthy between visits.

🦷

The 2-Minute Rule

Brush for exactly 2 minutes, twice a day. Use a timer — most people stop at 45 seconds. The difference is enormous for plaque control.

💧

Water After Every Meal

Can't brush after lunch? Rinse your mouth with water. It neutralizes acid and flushes food particles — a simple habit with big benefits.

🍓

Strawberries Are Not Whiteners

Despite the internet myth, strawberries contain malic acid that can erode enamel. Professional whitening is the only safe, effective method.

😴

Night Guards Save Teeth

Do you grind your teeth in your sleep? Bruxism damages enamel silently over years. A custom night guard protects thousands in future dental costs.

🧵

Floss Before You Brush

Flossing first loosens debris and plaque between teeth. Then brushing sweeps it all away. Switching the order makes brushing 40% more effective.

☕

Coffee and Teeth

Love your morning coffee? Use a straw to minimize contact with teeth, and rinse with water after. Don't brush for 30 minutes after acidic drinks.
